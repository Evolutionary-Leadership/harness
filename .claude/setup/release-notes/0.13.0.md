### Features
- Every issue your flow files now opens with a `## Why`: one or two
  sentences of prose saying what is wrong or missing today. `/to-tickets`,
  `/to-spec` and `/brainstorm` all write it, and `/feature` carries an idea
  issue's Why into the work item instead of composing a new one.
- `/to-tickets` checks each Why before the issue is created, and rewrites
  rather than files a ticket whose Why is missing, runs past two sentences,
  or just restates the title. It also shows you each Why during the
  granularity quiz, so you can correct one while the breakdown is still
  being agreed rather than after the issues exist.
- Idea issues from `/brainstorm` now have five sections rather than four,
  with the Why above Destination. Destination is where the idea is going;
  the Why is why anybody would.

### Notes
- The convention itself is written down in `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`,
  which is a write-once file. A project that already has that file keeps its
  own copy untouched by this upgrade, so the new section arrives only in
  projects scaffolded from now on. The skills above carry the rule on their
  own and work either way, but if you want the convention recorded where
  your team reads it, copy the section across by hand once.
- Nothing enforces the Why mechanically. There is no new checker, hook,
  label or issue field, and nothing added to your `check:` line. The rule
  lives inside the skills that write the bodies, which is where a rewrite is
  free; a check that failed after the issue existed would only teach people
  to file first and fix later.
